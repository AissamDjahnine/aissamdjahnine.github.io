eCAPRI

Clinical & Imaging Data for Post-TAVI Mortality Prediction

PJ. Lartaud, B. Harbaoui, A. Della Corte, A. Djahnine, O. Nempont, JM. Rouet, A. Cecilia Vlachomitrou, B. Riche, M. Rabilloud, S. Si-Mohamed, P. Douek, F. Derimay, G. Rioufol, P. Lantelme, L. Boussel

European Radiology · Published online 2025 · Volume 36 (2026)

Paper All publications All research

Overview

eCAPRI combines clinical information with automatically extracted CT biomarkers to estimate one-year mortality after transcatheter aortic valve implantation (TAVI).

Method

A deep learning model automates thoracic aortic calcium measurement. Additional imaging biomarkers are combined with the original CAPRI clinical variables. The score was developed on 765 patients and evaluated on 192 patients.

The added biomarkers include body-surface-area-indexed right-ventricle volume and pulmonary-artery maximum diameter, plus abdominal muscle area at L3. Biomarker selection removes redundant variables before evaluating candidate combinations.

Conclusions & limitations

Automated calcium measurement and additional CT biomarkers improve risk estimation in the evaluated cohort. Calcium segmentation can be affected by nearby calcifications and imaging artefacts. Broader population validation remains important.
