Pathology Detection & Localization in 3D Chest CT

Weakly-Supervised Learning

A. Djahnine, E. Jupin-delevaux, O. Nempont, F. Craighero, S. A. Si-Mohamed, V. Cottin, P. Douek, A. Popoff, L. Boussel

Medical Physics · 2024

Paper All publications All research

Overview

This study identifies multiple chest abnormalities and highlights their locations in 3D CT scans, combining patch-level predictions into a patient-level assessment.

Method

A ResNet-based patch classifier is pretrained with SimCLR on unannotated CT patches and fine-tuned with labeled examples. Abnormality probability maps across the volume are aggregated for multi-label classification.

The classifier distinguishes normal tissue from four abnormality categories. Sweeping it across a volume produces separate abnormality maps rather than a single scan label.

Conclusions & limitations

Patch predictions provide both patient-level decisions and spatial information. The variation across abnormalities motivates class-specific evaluation; localization findings should also be interpreted alongside the paper’s qualitative assessment.
