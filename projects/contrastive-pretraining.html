Contrastive Pretraining for Pulmonary Pathology Classification

Learning Representations from 3D CT Patches

A. Djahnine, Popoff Alexandre, E. Jupin-Delevaux, Cottin Vincent, Nempont Olivier, Boussel Loic

16th IEEE International Conference on Signal Processing (ICSP) · 2022

Paper All publications All research

Overview

This work explores contrastive pretraining for pulmonary pathology classification using three-dimensional chest CT patches.

Method

The research overview pairs a CT patch encoder with a SimCLR projection head for pretraining and a classification head for downstream evaluation. It compares transformations and weight-initialization strategies.

Two transformed views of a CT patch are used during contrastive pretraining. The projection head serves representation learning, while a separate classification head evaluates the learned features. The overview also visualizes the resulting patch embeddings.

Experiments

The research figure compares weight initialization, augmentation, and whether pretrained encoder weights remain frozen. Maximum and average weighted accuracy are reported separately.

Conclusions & limitations

Domain-specific representation learning is the central contribution. The comparison concerns 3D patch classification; it should not be read as a patient-level diagnostic accuracy result.
