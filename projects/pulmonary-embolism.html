Overview

This work investigates automated pulmonary embolism detection and severity assessment from three-dimensional CT pulmonary angiography examinations with limited annotations.

Method

The approach combines embolism detection with severity quantification using the Qanadli score and the right-to-left ventricle diameter ratio.

The pipeline has four outputs: clot detection, examination-level PE classification, Qanadli-score estimation, and RV/LV diameter-ratio prediction.

Conclusions & limitations

The study demonstrates feasibility of automated clot detection and severity estimation using blood-clot and cardiac segmentations. Further studies are needed to establish effectiveness in clinical practice.
