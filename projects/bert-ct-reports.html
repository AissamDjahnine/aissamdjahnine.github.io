Understanding French CT Reports with BERT

Measuring Pulmonary Embolism Positivity

E. Jupin-Delevaux, A. Djahnine, F. Talbot, A. Richard, S. Gouttard, A. Mansuy, P. Douek, S. A. Si-Mohamed, L. Boussel

Research in Diagnostic and Interventional Imaging (ReDII) · 2023

Paper All publications All research

Overview

This study uses natural language processing to analyze French CT reports and measure the diagnostic yield of examinations requested for suspected pulmonary embolism.

Method

A BERT-based sentence classifier distinguishes report content using three-category and eleven-category classification tasks. Report analysis identifies examinations with pulmonary embolism.

The three-category task separates non-thoracic, healthy thoracic, and pathological thoracic content. The finer classification task distinguishes thoracic findings; report-level analysis then identifies PE-positive examinations.

Conclusions & limitations

The classifier supports large-scale analysis of radiological reports and diagnostic yield. These results concern interpretation of report text, rather than direct detection of emboli in CT images.
